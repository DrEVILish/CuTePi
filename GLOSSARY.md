# CuTePi

Language for composing and playing a show.

## Language

**Show**:
The cues, groups, their order and the selected entry that an operator saves or loads together.

**Cue**:
An entry describing a file or live page and how it should play.

**Group**:
A named collection of cues or nested groups with its own place in the show.

**Armed cue**:
A cue whose playback has been prepared for a later GO. Being armed is distinct from measured first picture or sound latency.

**Prepared copy**:
A playback-oriented derivative of a media file. Having a prepared copy does not mean a cue is armed.

**Preparation job**:
Work that creates a prepared copy of one media file.
